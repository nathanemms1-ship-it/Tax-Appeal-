# TaxAppeal USA — Florida Google Ads Campaign Playbook
**Season: August 11 – September 18, 2026 | Budget Window: ~38 days**

---

## Why Google Ads, Why Now

Florida's TRIM notices land in mailboxes on August 11. The moment homeowners open that envelope and see a number that feels wrong, they search. That 5-week window — August 11 through September 18 — is one of the highest-intent, most time-compressed purchase cycles in consumer services. Nobody researches property tax appeals in January. They search when the clock is ticking.

The flat-rate model wins in paid search specifically because competitors' contingency pricing *sounds* free until the customer does the math. Your ad is the first place they see the comparison: "$89 flat" vs. "25–40% of your savings." On a $2,000 reduction that's the difference between paying $89 and paying $500–$800 every year. That math closes itself.

## 3. Landing Page Optimization

### What's Already Working (keep)
- Hero headline "Appeal Your Florida Property Taxes — $89 + County Fee" is clear and matches ad copy
- The fee transparency section (why Florida has a county fee) is excellent — it pre-answers the biggest objection
- Price comparison section (TaxAppeal vs. contingency firms) is a genuine conversion driver — the math is right there
- Testimonials with specific dollar amounts (Orange $2,200, Broward $1,960, Palm Beach $3,100) are strong proof
- The filing window urgency bar (August 11 → September 18) is smart and already live

### Changes Made (code deployed)
**UTM parameter preservation**: When a visitor clicks from a Google Ad, the UTM params and gclid are captured in sessionStorage so they survive through the 4-step /apply flow. This is critical for accurate attribution — without it, Google can't match ad spend to conversions.

**State pre-fill**: All florida.js CTAs now pass `?state=FL` to `/apply`, so the state field defaults to Florida. This removes one data-entry step from the funnel, which matters on mobile.

### Recommended Changes (not yet in code — do when ready)
1. **Above-fold trust signals**: Add "As seen in" or review platform badges if you have them. The 82% stat in the stats row is good but unattributed.
2. **Mobile CTA stickiness**: On mobile, the "Start my dispute" button disappears as you scroll. Consider a sticky bottom bar on mobile during the filing window.
3. **TRIM Notice photo**: Add a screenshot or illustration of a TRIM notice with an arrow pointing to the assessed value line. Paid traffic converts better with visual confirmation that you understand their exact problem.
4. **Specific call to action for search intent**: Visitors from "trim notice appeal florida" are holding their TRIM notice right now. A subheadline like "Just received your TRIM notice? You have 25 days — here's how it works" would speak directly to that moment.

## 4. Conversion Tracking Setup

### What Was Built (code deployed to repo)

Three files were updated:

**`_app.js`** — Google tag loads on every page:
```
NEXT_PUBLIC_GTAG_ID=G-XXXXXXXXXX   (your GA4 Measurement ID)
NEXT_PUBLIC_GADS_ID=AW-XXXXXXXXXX  (your Google Ads Conversion ID)
```

**`apply.js`** — `begin_checkout` fires when user clicks "File my dispute · $89" (after agreeing to terms). This is a micro-conversion that helps Google learn what kinds of searchers reach the checkout stage.

**`success.js`** — `purchase` fires when the /success page loads with a verified Stripe session. This is the primary conversion for Google Ads ROAS tracking. The `transaction_id` (Stripe session ID) prevents double-counting if the user refreshes the page.

### Steps to Activate (one-time setup in Google Ads + Vercel)

**Step 1: Create a Google Ads account** (if you don't have one)
- Go to ads.google.com → New account → Smart campaign → switch to Expert Mode immediately

**Step 2: Create conversion actions**
- In Google Ads: Tools → Conversions → + New conversion action → Website
- Create two conversions:
  1. **"Begin checkout"** — Category: Submit lead form, Value: $0 (micro-conversion, no value)
  2. **"Purchase — $89"** — Category: Purchase, Value: $89 (primary conversion)
- For each, copy the Conversion ID (AW-XXXXXXXXXX) and Conversion Label (looks like ABCD1234efgh)

**Step 3: Add to Vercel environment variables**
```
NEXT_PUBLIC_GTAG_ID=G-XXXXXXXXXX
NEXT_PUBLIC_GADS_ID=AW-XXXXXXXXXX
NEXT_PUBLIC_GADS_CHECKOUT_LABEL=your_checkout_label_here
NEXT_PUBLIC_GADS_PURCHASE_LABEL=your_purchase_label_here
```
Deploy. The tracking goes live immediately on the next deploy.

**Step 4: Verify tracking in Google Tag Assistant**
- Install Google Tag Assistant Chrome extension
- Visit taxappealusa.com/florida → click the CTA → go through apply → the Tag Assistant should show a gtag hit
- Complete a test purchase through Stripe test mode → verify a purchase event fires on /success

**Step 5: Set primary/secondary conversion in Google Ads**
- Mark "Purchase — $89" as Primary (used for bidding)
- Mark "Begin checkout" as Secondary (informational only)

## 6. Budget & Performance Benchmarks

### Starting Budget: $30/day
At $30/day over 38 days = ~$1,140 total spend. At a 3% conversion rate and a $3 CPC:
- ~380 clicks → ~11–12 purchases
- Revenue: ~$1,000–$1,100
- Roughly break-even on direct ROAS — but the data collected powers the much larger Texas season starting April 2027

### When to Scale
- If CPA drops below $40 by day 10, increase to $60/day
- If TRIM search volume spikes (watch Google Ads search impression share), push to $100/day in the final 10 days before Sep 18

### What to Watch (daily during the window)
- **CPC**: Florida property tax terms should be $2–5 per click. If you're seeing $8+, tighten match types or increase quality score with better ad copy
- **CTR**: Aim for 5%+ on TRIM notice exact match. Below 2% means the ad copy isn't matching intent
- **Conversion rate on /apply**: If people reach /apply but don't finish, it's a funnel issue, not an ads issue — the apply flow audit should come first
- **Search term report (check weekly)**: Add any irrelevant terms that appear as negatives immediately. Watch for "how to do it myself" terms consuming budget

## 7. Post-Season Setup (building toward Texas 2027)

The Florida window is 38 days. The learning data it generates — which search terms convert, what CPC is sustainable, what CTA language beats others — is more valuable than the revenue. Texas starts April 1, 2027, with a much larger homeowner population and no county VAB filing fee (simpler offer). Run Florida even at near-break-even to get the attribution model trained.

After Florida closes September 18:
- Pause the FL campaign (don't delete — preserve the conversion history)
- Export top-converting search terms for TX keyword list
- Build a TX remarketing audience from FL visitors (property tax appeal intent doesn't expire quickly)
- Set a calendar reminder to reactivate + build TX campaigns by March 1, 2027
